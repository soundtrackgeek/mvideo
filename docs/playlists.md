# Starter playlists

Created from the live 15,559-video catalog on 2026-10-01. Each entry below matched an available video by artist and title. Genres are editorial selections; they are not inferred tags for every song by an artist. Artists are interleaved to make a running order.

The chart selection uses **UK Official Singles Chart Top 10** as its inclusion rule. Peak positions refer to the song, while the app keeps the video’s existing catalog year. No filename or source media was changed.

Recipes are in `server/mvideo/data/starter-playlists.json`. `mvideo seed-playlists` previews matches; `mvideo seed-playlists --apply` saves them once. Renamed, edited or deleted starter playlists are not overwritten or recreated by rerunning the command. Missing songs are reported and omitted.
